## Solution (spoiler)

1. **Look** or **Use** the desk → take the brass key  
2. **Use** the brass key **with** the chest → take the locket  

---

## What’s inside

- Room picture + key/locket icons (`assets/`)
- Music and short sound effects
- A quest, hints, an achievement, and autosave
- A tiny “room settle” creak when you enter
